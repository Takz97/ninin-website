Privacy

Your writing is yours.

ninin is designed around a local-first writing workflow.

Current desktop release

Ordinary writing and project work are designed to run locally on your Windows computer. No online account is required for the core writing workflow.

Backups

Because local data belongs to the device and app environment, regular backups and exports are strongly recommended. Future online services, if introduced, will be covered by updated privacy information.
