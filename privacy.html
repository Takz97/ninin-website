Privacy

Your writing is yours.

ninin is designed around a local-first writing workflow.

Current desktop release

The core writing and project workflow is designed to run locally on your Windows computer. An online account is not required to use the core writing features.

Backups

Because your writing is stored locally, we strongly recommend keeping regular backups of your important projects and using the available export and backup features.

If online services or account-based features are introduced in future releases, the privacy information will be updated to explain what information is collected, why it is collected, and how it is handled.
