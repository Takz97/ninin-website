Installation

Getting started with ninin.

A simple guide to installing ninin on your Windows computer.

1. Download ninin

Download the official ninin Windows installer from our website when it becomes available.

2. Run the installer

Open the downloaded installer and follow the on-screen instructions. You can choose where ninin is installed.

3. Open ninin

Once installation is complete, open ninin from your Windows Start menu or desktop shortcut.

4. Protect your writing

Keep regular backups of your writing projects. We recommend storing backup copies somewhere separate from your main working files.
